GURU 3.2 - PRESENTATION / LATENCY FIX

1. Screen sharing no longer requests 1080p30.
2. Sender output is limited to 15 FPS and scaled to ~1280x720.
3. Video bitrate is capped dynamically:
   - 1 remote peer: 900 Kbps
   - 2-3 remote peers: 650 Kbps per peer
   - 4+ remote peers: 450 Kbps per peer
4. Audio sender is marked high priority and capped at 64 Kbps.
5. Screen video is low priority and uses balanced degradation.
6. Current Guru tab is excluded from Chromium share picker when supported.
7. Presentation fills the available meeting area.
8. Participant cards float over the presentation instead of shrinking it.
9. Video element uses intrinsic dimensions + max bounds so the full source is visible.
10. Blur cost is reduced while presentation mode is active.

IMPORTANT:
Avoid sharing the browser window that is displaying Guru itself. Doing that creates
an infinite mirror effect and can consume substantial GPU/encoder/network resources.
